# Single-cell informed metabolic modeling reveals organ-specific metabolic adaptations in breast cancer organotropism
## Garhima Arora, Samrat Chatterjee

## DOI: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.01.748342

We have developed a computational pipeline to integrate single cell RNA seq data with genome scale metabolic models to build epithelial cell specific metabolic models to study breast cancer organotropism. Our single-cell-informed metabolic modeling approach shows that breast cancer metastasis is associated with both shared and organ-specific metabolic adaptations, and identify potential metabolic vulnerabilities that could be targeted to treat breast cancer progression. The developed systems level approach can be utilized to characterize the metabolic shifts that epithelial cells undergo during other cancer metastatsis.
